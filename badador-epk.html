About

Badador (Bahador Qorashi) is an Iranian live modular techno performer crafting beat-driven grooves through improvisational hardware sequencing.

Based in Tehran, he has played in Pisa, Saint Petersburg, Moscow, Goa, Istanbul, and Baku — his most frequent European home being Club Temple in Istanbul. Since 2020 he runs Sanati Records, releasing Iranian and international underground artists; his compilations have been featured on Bandcamp New & Notable.

His sets push analog machines into generative territory, designed for clubs and festivals seeking something beyond the DJ booth.
